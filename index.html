Resilience lives in people, and in the communities around them.

We study how individuals and communities withstand, recover, and grow in the face of adversity. Our work spans personal trauma and community-wide disaster.

Research

Resilience at two levels, and the growth between them

Recovery after adversity is never only an individual story or only a community story. Our research examines resilience at both levels: how people intentionally grow, and how the places they live shape that growth. Posttraumatic growth (PTG) serves as a central outcome connecting the two.

Individual resilience & growth

How people adapt and intentionally grow after trauma and adversity, including personal growth initiative (PGI), meaning-making, rumination, and coping. We also ask when reported posttraumatic growth reflects genuine change.

Community resilience

How community resources, social cohesion, and place shape recovery after hurricanes, wildfires, and public health crises. We ask why some communities fare better than their circumstances predict.

Methods and measurement

Rigorous methods to connect the two levels, including structural equation modeling, latent growth and mixture modeling, multilevel modeling, and intensive longitudinal (daily diary) designs.
